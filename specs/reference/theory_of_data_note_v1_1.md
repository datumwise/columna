## Abstract

We propose that data constitutes an autonomous abstract realm with
intrinsic laws — not an epiphenomenon of the physical world whose
meaning must be imported through a mapping to external referents. We
give the realm a two-level structure: the **particle** — one typed value at one anchor point, a key-value
pair whose key is a location in a typed coordinate structure; the
elementary observation, below which there is no data; and
the **atom**, an (anchor, vector) pair — the column, understood as
particles bound by a shared key-structure: the smallest unit of data
with internal structure, and therefore the smallest with stable
identity and lawful behavior. Meaning is an emergent property of
the binding: a variable's aggregation family, basis, grain, and unit
are questions about how its vector behaves along its anchor —
undefined at particle level, well-defined at atom level. The realm's
dynamics are two primitive morphisms classified by what they do to the
anchor — the mapper (anchor-preserving, lifting a value-function across
the vector) and the reducer (anchor-coarsening, an aggregator collapsing
the vector to a coarser coordinate system) — and lawfulness of
composition is decided where the aggregator's algebra meets the
variable's declared law: its measure family, its operator-indexed
B-anchor rules, its place in a corroborated dimension hierarchy, its
universe and that universe's law of existence. On this
foundation, meaning itself is defined as a two-arrow mapping: from
expressions of a grammar generated by a declared model, through the
model, to the world of particles — the first arrow total by
construction, the second certified by adjudication against the data.
We state the position's epistemic rank honestly: a working theory,
externally confirmed by independent rediscovery in four traditions,
with a falsifiable edge. The practical consequence is a diagnosis: the
row-and-table paradigm took the bookkeeping unit as the semantic unit,
leaving meaning homeless — which is why analytical systems fail
silently, why declared-semantics interchange formats cannot adjudicate
even in principle, and why the columnar movement, having converged on
the atom's shape for efficiency reasons, is one layer from complete.

## 1 · The question and the two-level answer

The founding question is old and plain: *what is data?* The answer we
take seriously: data is recorded observation. From it, a two-level
structure follows.

**The particle — one typed value at one anchor point.** The elementary
observation, a key-value pair whose key is not an unspecified database
key but a location in a typed coordinate structure: *this* variable
took *this* typed value at *this* anchor point. Genuinely elementary —
below it there is no data, only un-referenced numbers — and its
semantic status is exact: **locally meaningful but analytically
simple**. A particle carries its own typed, located meaning; what it
cannot carry are the analytical questions — variation, hierarchy
movement, missingness, aggregation, blocked reduction — which are
undefined for a single observation and become defined only at the
atom. Key-value and document stores hold particles honestly: local
meaning intact, analytical law absent — which is why no semantic layer
has ever been built on one; there is no chemistry in a particle
accelerator.

**The atom — (anchor, vector).** The column: particles bound by a
shared key-structure — one anchor, one meaning, a vector of values
indexed by it — equivalently, and in its sharpest form, **a typed
partial function from anchor points to values**, and identically **the
homogeneous, functionally consistent binding of its particles**: one
value type, one anchor, one universe, one functional rule; the anchor
is the domain, the vector its graph, and functional consistency — one
value per anchor point — is the binding's law. An atom in the chemist's sense: not the
smallest thing, but the smallest thing with **internal structure** —
organized multiplicity, many observations under one binding — where a
particle is *simple*: form without parts. Identity and lawfulness are
structure's consequences: identity is what the binding persists as,
and law governs relations among parts, so only structured things can
be lawful — the smallest structured unit is therefore also the
smallest with stable identity and lawful behavior.
**Meaning is an emergent property of the binding.** Whether a variable
is intensive or extensive; whether it is a stock or a flow; what its
grain is; what happens to it under aggregation along a hierarchy —
these are questions about the vector's behavior along its anchor. They
are undefined for a single (key, value) and well-defined for the
column. The properties analytical correctness depends on live at the
atom, not the particle, and not the row.

**The anchor's own structure, in one breath**: dimensions are typed
sets; an axis is a hierarchy of compatible dimensions under declared
coarsening maps (Hour → Day → Month → Year → AllTime); an anchor
selects one level from every axis; an anchor point is one tuple of the
resulting product — and every axis terminates in a singleton level, so
**an axis omitted from an abbreviated anchor has not disappeared: it
has been contracted to its terminal point.** The grand total is not a
special case; it is the anchor at every terminal.

**Above the atom**, composition continues lawfully: the **frame**
(columns bound by a shared anchor — the molecule; alignment is the
bond, derivation the molecular behavior); **universes** (typed populations
with lawful crossings between them — phases, with declared crossing
semantics as the boundary chemistry, and each with its own law of
existence, §3); and the
**transmutation law** — what happens to an atom when its anchor
changes, the input-grain/output-grain distinction that makes metric
expression governable at all (developed in the Two Anchors deposit).

**Consequences settled by the two-level form.** A *row* is a particle
collision event: several variables observed at coincident coordinates.
Record it however convenient — that is bookkeeping. A *table* is a
recording convention, not a semantic unit — and the industry's oldest
term quietly misassigns the noun: "structured data" locates the
structure in the table, when a table's arrangement is tabulation —
bookkeeping order — while structure in the sense that supports
identity and law begins one level down, at the column. The
structured/unstructured divide is drawn at the wrong boundary: the
structure that matters — the one laws can govern — starts where
observations organize under a binding. The row-based era's
original sin was ontological: it took the bookkeeping unit as the
semantic unit, leaving meaning nowhere to live but prose — a
homelessness inherited by every metrics layer built on tables. And the
columnar formats' record batch is, in this notation,
(anchor-implicit, vectors): the wire converged on the atom's shape.

## 2 · The dynamics: two primitive morphisms

The statics say what the things are; the dynamics say what may happen
to them. At the particle level, behavior is ordinary: functions act on
values, typed by the particle's value-type — `f : i_val → o_val`, no
anchor in sight. At the atom level, the primitive operations are
classified **by what they do to the anchor**, and there are exactly two:

**The mapper — anchor-preserving.** A value-function lifted pointwise
across the vector: `map(f) : (A, v) → (A, f ∘ v)`. The atom changes
substance and never coordinates; derivation of new columns from old
ones is mappers over aligned atoms.

**The reducer — anchor-coarsening.** A lawful coarsening partitions
the atom's support into **fibers** — the bag of particles sharing each
coarse anchor point — and an aggregator `g` collapses each fiber:
`reduce(g) : (A, v) → (A′, v′)`, where `A → A′` is a lawful coarsening
and the output value-type may differ from the input's. The reducer's
signature carries **two anchors** — the input grain and the output
grain — and this is not a notational convenience: the two-anchors
thesis (developed in its own deposit) is the reducer's type read aloud,
and the anchor is thereby revealed as the realm's *type system*: these
two signatures are its typing rules, and an adjudicating kernel is a
type checker whose types are meanings.

**The aggregator algebra.** Aggregators are of many kinds, and their
properties are where decades of practitioner folklore compress into
law. Three properties carry most of the weight. *Monoidality*: is `g`
associative and commutative with an identity? Sum, min, max, and count
are — which is exactly what makes them freely re-aggregable and safe
under partial aggregation. *Decomposability*: mean is not a monoid but
factors exactly through one (carry sum and count jointly, divide at the
end); count-distinct does not factor at all without summary structures,
which is why honest systems compute it over sketches. *Composition
stability*: does `reduce(g, A → A″)` equal
`reduce(g, A′ → A″) ∘ reduce(g, A → A′)` for an intermediate `A′`? For
monoidal aggregators, yes — a sum of sums is the sum. For the mean,
**no**: a mean of means is a different statistic than the mean — and
this one-line theorem of the algebra is the entire class of silent
analytical betrayal in miniature. The companion deposit's executed
attack — a lawful-looking plan diverging 13–17% monthly from the
faithful answer by interposing one undeclared intermediate grain — is
this theorem running on public data.

The further operations — restriction, reindexing, lag, windows,
allocation, alignment, expansion, crossing — are not additional
primitives and are no longer left open: they are **constructions in a
layered calculus** of anchors, atoms, frames, and universes, developed
in the technical companion. The division of labor is exact and worth
stating as law: **anchor operations determine which particles meet;
mappers and reducers determine what their values may become.** Motion
machinery and value machinery are different faculties — and the
value-level finer direction remains as closed as ever: no mapper or
reducer can invent rows the data does not distinguish; expansion in
the calculus arranges which particles meet a computation, it never
manufactures values.

## 3 · Where algebra meets declaration: family, hierarchy, universe, basis

The aggregator algebra states what *can* compose; it cannot state what
*may* compose for a particular variable — that is declared, per atom,
and adjudicated against the particles. Column and operator are
therefore one framework, not two: an atom without operator law is a
label; an operator without declared atoms is a calculator; meaning is
the join. The declarations that carry the law follow.

**The three anchors of a column — V, M, B.** The declaration layer
begins where §1 ended: the anchor is the atom's domain, and it turns
out to be the first of **three** anchors every column carries — *where
it lives, where it leaks, where it blocks*. The **V-anchor** (value
anchor) is the domain itself: the coordinate set over which the column
is a function, with the complete grain being the pair (anchor,
universe). The **M-anchor** (missingness anchor) declares the
*mechanism* behind the column's absent values — and its jurisdiction
is grounded in the universe's **law of existence**: in an *event*
universe, occurrences generate the points, so absence of a point is
non-occurrence; in a *spine* universe, membership is established
independently of whether a value is present, so absence of a value at
a member point *speaks* — it is a gap with a mechanism. What an
aggregate over leaky data means depends on which law governs and why
the leaks exist; a reduction that ignores its operand's missingness
mechanism is accounting fiction. The **B-anchor** (blocked anchor) is *prohibitive
and operator-indexed*: for each operator, it names the axes along
which that reduction is forbidden outright — not "handle with care"
but "no lawful roll-up exists here" — so a variable may lawfully sum
along one axis, average along it, and be blocked from summing along
another, each ruled per operator.
Together the triad is the column's complete legal identity: domain,
leak law, and block law, each a first-class declaration, each
adjudicable, each feeding what may be served and what must be
disclosed or refused.

**The measure family** is the atom's declared aggregation law: which
aggregators, along which dimensions, preserve the variable's meaning.
An extensive measure (revenue, units) sums along population
dimensions; an intensive one (a rate, a ratio, a temperature) does
not; a derived measure (average order value = revenue over orders) is
non-additive by construction. Family and algebra must *both* license a
composition: a monoidal aggregator applied against a non-additive
family is algebraically clean and semantically unlawful — the algebra
permits, the family forbids, and the refusal stands.

**The dimension hierarchy** is where reducers are allowed to move.
Anchors live in a coarsening lattice — day to month to quarter, store
to region — and the lattice's edges are not assumed: they are declared
and then **corroborated against the particles at publish** (does every
store in fact reach exactly one region?). A transport is a reduction
along a corroborated edge; an uncorroborated edge is not a smaller
guarantee but a refused one.

**The universe** is a population of observation — one anchor system,
one phase: transactions and inventory are different universes even
when they mention the same stores. Within a universe the unary
calculus applies; *between* universes lies a boundary operation —
crossing, governed by declared faces with their own spend law —
deliberately outside this note's primitives, developed in the
multi-universe deposit.

**And a correction this revision makes openly: basis is not a
primitive.** Earlier drafts declared stock-versus-flow as a
first-class temporal declaration; the technical companion shows it is
a *domain explanation* of something more general — **patterns of
operator-indexed B-anchor rules over typed axes**. A flow (revenue in
January) is a variable whose sum is unblocked along time; a stock
(inventory on January 31) is one whose sum is blocked there while
other operators remain lawful — and a stock summed across a year is
still not a number plus a caveat; it is not a number. The folklore
survives intact as the B-anchor's most familiar exhibit; what it loses
is primitive status.

These declarations — the V/M/B triad, the family, the hierarchy, the
universe with its law of existence — are the load-bearing vocabulary of the companion
system's declaration language, and together with §2's algebra they
close the loop the note opened. **The closure is worth stating
plainly, at working-theory rank: lawful transformation is now
conceptually definable.** A motion is lawful iff every clause with
jurisdiction licenses it: its anchor behavior is well-typed (mapper
preserves; reducer coarsens along a corroborated lattice edge); the
aggregator's algebra supports the composition; the measure family
permits it; no B-anchor blocks the axis; the basis is respected on
temporal dimensions; the M-anchor's mechanism is accounted in what the
aggregate may claim; and the motion stays within one universe or
crosses only through a declared face. Meaning attaches at the binding
(§1); the binding has two primitive motions (§2); and every motion is
judged at every clause (§3) before anything is served. That
conjunction — checkable, per-atom, against declarations that were
themselves tried against the particles — is what this note means by a
theory of data: not a metaphor for care, but a definition of lawful
movement decidable relative to its declared premises.

## 4 · Autonomy: data is not an epiphenomenon

Row and object ontologies — including the enterprise "ontology"
platforms — are ontologies **of the physical world**, with data
treated as its image: reality has objects; data mirrors them; data's
structure is derivative and its meaning must be imported through the
mapping (documentation, lineage, referents). An epiphenomenon cannot
have laws of its own, so no one built meaning a house.

This note asserts the realm's autonomy: **recorded observation is an
abstract world in itself, with intrinsic laws never inherited from the
things observed.** The founding gesture has precedent. Computation was
"just what machines do" until Turing gave the abstract realm its own
theory, with laws silicon realizes but does not determine. Information
was "properties of signals" until Shannon axiomatized it — and Shannon
founded his theory by explicitly bracketing semantics. **The theory of
data is the bracketed half**: data-as-meaning given its own laws, as
Shannon gave them to data-as-signal.

**The autonomy signature is empirical: the laws contain no domain
knowledge.** A mean of intermediate sums misstates a mean of atoms in
retail, epidemiology, and astronomy identically; grain transmutation,
crossing conservation, and family bounds know nothing of customers or
revenue-as-referent, and adjudicate models they have never seen.
Domain-independence is the experiment that separates intrinsic law
from borrowed structure — and world-mapped ontologies cannot exhibit
it, since their rules are about their referents by construction.

## 5 · Meaning as a mapping

With the realm in place, meaning takes its classical definition — an
interpretation function (the Tarski/Montague tradition) — with the
target changed:

> expression ──(arrow 1)──▶ declared model ──(arrow 2)──▶ the world
> of particles

**Arrow 1 is total and mentionful by construction** when the grammar
is *generated from* the declared model: every expression speaks the
target's native vocabulary (anchors, measures, coordinates); no
expression exists without a target. **Arrow 2 is certified by
adjudication**: the declared model is checked against the particles
before anything is served; verdicts are the certification of the
second arrow. Meaning is the composite — and what a system may say
about its own arrows is its honesty contract: *served* (the arrow
lands cleanly), *disclosed* (it lands, with conditions), *clarified*
(several lawful arrows exist — choose), *refused* (**no arrow exists
into the lawful world**).

The contrast with current practice is now exact, broken at both ends
of the other arrow. Source-side: SQL is a grammar of containers —
tables, joins, slots — whose expressions never mention what meaning
is about; grains, populations, and families appear nowhere in the
syntax, so interpretation must conjure them from context. Target-side:
the physical world has no laws of data — referents carry no
aggregation rules — so mapping into it cannot yield lawful meaning,
only asserted meaning. **A semantic layer maps container-queries onto
a lawless target; the theory maps data-expressions onto data's own
world, whose laws are declared, executable, and checked.** In
linguistic terms the framework carries all three classical layers —
generated syntax, adjudicated semantics, and honest pragmatics
(answers as speech acts with felicity conditions) — where the
products named "semantic layers" carry none.

## 6 · Independent rediscovery (the external confirmations)

Discovered structures leave signatures. **(i) Convergence from four
traditions**: statistics has taken the variable as its atom for a
century; dimensional modeling's grain discipline groped toward the
same joints from practice; the columnar-engine lineage converged on
the atom's shape from performance pressure (vectorization works
because columns are semantically homogeneous — the efficiency was the
shadow of the ontology); and type-level grain formalization arrived
independently from formal methods in 2026 (arXiv:2601.00995). **(ii)
Compression**: decades of trap folklore — fan traps, mean-of-sums,
stock-versus-flow, allocation drift — compress into a small closed
set of declarable per-variable laws; a public catalog of 67 silent
failure modes maps onto them. **(iii) Resistance of alternatives**:
governance attempted at the table or metric level keeps failing
identically — in controlled form, nine frontier AI models failed the
same benchmark families regardless of vendor, scale, or
documentation; in the ecosystem, current interchange formats carry
metrics as per-dialect SQL strings with no neutral expression form,
so cross-dialect consistency is unverifiable within the format even
in principle, and independent implementations that approach the
measure algebra stub or annotate precisely where the laws would have
to be. The wall keeps being discovered and marked with signs; the
signs are not the wall.

## 7 · Rank, edge, and program

**Rank**: a working theory — internally closed (the derivation chain:
what-is-data → particle → atom → the two morphisms and their algebra →
per-variable declared law (the anchor triad, family, hierarchy, universe and its law of existence) →
frames → universes → adjudication → honesty contract → a generated
grammar), externally confirmed as above, and deliberately modest about
its metaphors: particle/atom/molecule/phase is teaching scaffolding
borrowed from chemistry, not the substance. **Falsifiable edge**: the
claim's scope is analytical data — measurements over populations. If
some data genuinely resists per-variable meaning, the atom has a
boundary there; the claim is held lightly beyond that scope.
**Program**: the technical companion carries the full typed
construction — coordinate structure, universes and their laws of
existence, the calculi, and the lawfulness conjunction in formal
dress; the companion system implements the theory (declared
models adjudicated against data; answers in four moods; a grammar
generated per model), the companion benchmark measures the failure
families the theory predicts, and the Open Planner deposit extends
the theory to certified analytical plans from untrusted searchers.
The practical prediction worth stating plainly: as declared-semantics
interchange succeeds industry-wide, the world fills with portable,
standardized, unverified declarations — and value migrates to
whatever can adjudicate them. That is the layer this theory exists to
govern.
