# Frame-QL Companion-Corpus Assessment Against ToD v7.1

**Assessment v0.1 — 7 September 2026**  
**Comparison target:** *The Theory of Data*, Version 7.1, Full Manuscript Working Draft 0.4.  
**Status:** Source assessment and recommendations only. No companion source, theory manuscript, repository, grammar, capability registry, profile, runtime, or deployment was changed.

## Executive assessment

The principal Frame-QL architecture remains compatible with the v7.1 manuscript: a request identifies analytical results; governed law determines their meaning; trusted planning and execution realize them. One current anchor, explicit constitutive input anchors, governed ambiguity resolution, co-participation, restricted physical authority, and the distinction between output aliases and analytical identity all survive.

The companion corpus is not yet a consistent current specification. Its most direct conflicts are in the recent working documents we produced during the theory's evolution—not in the retrieved passages of the older published introductions. Several working documents still deny FIRST/LAST family standing. The early note titled “O2 Reconciliation to Theory of Data v7.1” corrects that exclusion but retains the subsequently withdrawn universal projection-fiber-locality test and an unfinished TOP-k extension. The accompanying migration plans and acceptance cases carry the same assumptions.

The required response is a bounded semantic reconciliation, not a new ontology pass and not a requirement to implement all of v7.1 before publishing the theory. The revised companions must distinguish family admission, the retained representation's reuse capability, and language/profile availability. They must also preserve the newer evidence result: a sufficient basis need not be the only argument establishing its target, and a complete target need not require every candidate intermediate plan to be executable.

**Disposition:** retain the request-language architecture; replace or explicitly supersede contradictory working guidance before presenting it as the current companion specification. Preserve historical publications and snapshot reconnaissance as history. Do not turn an editorial correction into shipped syntax or implementation authority.

## 2. What should be preserved

### The request/result boundary

PI's retrieved abstract and scope distinguish analytical declaration from physical manufacture. They explicitly keep formal grammar and shipped meaning separate from the theoretical explanation. PP similarly preserves the requester/adjudicator/executor distinction and does not promise a number merely because a request parses. These passages remain useful entry points. They need a carefully scoped successor reference, not replacement by the full witness proof.

### One current anchor and constitutive inner anchors

The local language's distinction between `@` expression anchoring and final-frame `AT` remains compatible with T §§2–3. An inner anchor may distinguish the constructed family without becoming a second current anchor of each resulting measure. This conceptual compatibility does not make every mathematical anchored expression valid shipped syntax.

### Formation, co-participation, and structural alignment

LQ §§4.4–4.7 preserve pointwise co-participation and broadcast as availability of an already-established coarse value in a finer enclosing expression. Broadcast does not manufacture a finer measure of that family or allocate a total. LQ §2.4 correctly separates input restriction from output selection. These recovered distinctions should survive reconciliation.

### Resolved meaning rather than physical convention

O1's useful core is that shorthand may omit what governance determines uniquely, while resolved meaning cannot omit meaning-bearing choices. O2's distinction between analytical sequence points and physical rows, and its focal context/neighborhood analysis, remain useful independently of its superseded admission claims. O3's sparse order construction and planner/executor boundary are particularly close to the present manuscript.

### Separate kinds of authority

The public profiles and generated-status document already distinguish authored obligations from recorded build coverage. Historical notes should remain history. This structure should be preserved; the review does not justify another monolithic document that blends theory, grammar, profile promises, and observed behavior.

## 3. Finding F1 — The recent language and order drafts retain the withdrawn family exclusion

**Observed.** LQ §3.6, §9.1, §9.11, §10.3, §13, and its §14 acceptance table direct FIRST/LAST into a non-family category. O1 §2 and O2 §§9.1, 26–27, and the conclusion repeat the categorical boundary. These are operative rules and acceptance expectations, not only historical descriptions.

**Comparison.** T §§7–8 explicitly develops the governed ordered construction, independently constituted witness family W, and scalar target family L. Analytical-point-order dependence does not itself prohibit family standing. Family admission still requires the complete target/law contract.

**Consequence.** An implementer following the older notes could reject a valid constructed family or redesign the capability system around a distinction the theory no longer makes. Relabeling every LAST as a valid family would be the opposite error: a callable spelling is not a complete family contract.

**Recommended correction.** Replace the categorical passages and their negative acceptance expectations with references to T's actual admission contract. Preserve the no-physical-order rule, analytical formation before selection, and separation of output ordering. Do not claim that any legacy `.last` path now satisfies v7.1 merely because the family construction is theoretically admitted.

**Priority:** required before these working notes are used as current implementation guidance.

## 4. Finding F2 — The early v7.1 reconciliation is itself superseded

**Observed.** OR §§3 and 7 make projection-fiber locality a general admission condition and reject ordinary focal constructions because their values reach beyond the focal identity fiber. OR §6 advances TOP-k as an admissible-in-principle family construction. Its program summary labels the associated early theory and stress-test work complete.

**Comparison.** T §3.4 distinguishes contextual formation from continuation over established contributions. Appendix C.4 explicitly withdraws the universal formation-locality exclusion. The publication-shape draft keeps TOP-k outside the admitted foundation matrix and moves statistical detail into a bounded reference supplement.

**Consequence.** The title “reconciliation to v7.1” makes OR particularly likely to be mistaken for the latest rule, even though it freezes an intermediate position that the full manuscript corrected.

**Recommended correction.** Explicitly supersede the affected OR sections. Use T §3.4's compact contextual-family contract: specified target and formation, participation and defined/empty cases, an adequate independently establishable basis, and coherent admitted continuation that does not silently change formation. Ordinary LAG or RANK syntax grants no such continuation by itself; contextual ancestry is also not an automatic disqualification. Keep TOP-k as an unadmitted extension observation outside this reconciliation's work program.

**Priority:** required semantic correction; not a new theory pass.

## 5. Finding F3 — Family admission and representation-specific re-entry must not share one classification

**Observed.** CP §5 proposes treating re-entry certification as inapplicable to FIRST/LAST because they are non-family ordered expressions. Its §16 acceptance list requires the corresponding category change. LQ §13 has the same direction. This is a consequence of the withdrawn exclusion, not an independent justification for changing the schema.

**Comparison.** T §§8 and 10 distinguish W from L. W can supply exact admitted witness continuation; scalar L generally does not retain that state. L remains a family. COUNT likewise distinguishes continuing already-established counts from counting displayed inputs (T §5.2).

**Consequence.** Neither of the following is valid:

- a non-re-entrant displayed result is therefore not a family;
- a result's family standing means its displayed carrier is self-sufficient.

**Recommended correction.** Preserve separate judgments about operation shape, use of analytical order, family-law admission, the actual retained input's sufficiency for a proposed operation, and profile/build availability. This is a set of questions, not a prescribed set of new enum fields. Rebase the migration plan before asking CC to choose a schema. Do not simply flip FIRST/LAST re-entry from false to true.

The public capability specification provides a useful cross-check: FIRST/LAST are recorded as ratified with re-entry disabled because the finalized value lacks the order witness; COUNT also distinguishes intake from continuation. These observations do not certify the implementation's full ordered-family law. [WEB-K]

**Priority:** required before capability-schema or runtime-category migration.

## 6. Finding F4 — The family-law checklist and promotion chapter need the present target-specification rule

**Observed.** LQ §6.5 lists canonical form, operands, participation, types, state, and continuation, but not T §4's explicit target-specification obligation. LQ §3.5 allows admitted law expressions to establish or denote a family, while §11.1 broadly states that a lawful Frame-QL expression can exist only for its request.

**Comparison.** T §§2.2, 3.7, 3.9, 4, and 5.3 distinguish the quantity's definition, its canonical expression or name, adequate bases, and authority to register/publish a durable object. A nominated non-circular defining construction can specify the target; output agreement cannot supply its definition afterward.

**Consequence.** A future implementer could mistake a list of bases for the target definition, or interpret the promotion boundary as denying the ability of a canonical expression to denote a family already established by an admitted law.

**Recommended correction.** Reference the single ToD family contract rather than maintaining a shorter divergent checklist. Say that `AS`, query-local `WITH`, and saving query text do not themselves publish or ratify a new family. Separately say that an admitted canonical family construction can denote analytical identity without a business noun or a new naming ceremony. Query denotation and publication authority are different acts.

**Priority:** a small but necessary language-contract clarification.

## 7. Finding F5 — “Zero, one, many” must count meanings, not available implementation routes

**Observed.** LQ §10.1 already says it counts distinct lawful meanings and groups candidates proven equivalent. It also lists ambiguous governed paths as an application. The principle is sound; the new sufficient-state account needs an explicit application note.

**Comparison.** T §§3.8, 5.3–5.5, 6.4, and 10.5 separate constitutive identity from a choice of basis or realization.

**Risk, not a reproduced defect.** Two adequate bases for one fixed target could be treated as two meanings and unnecessarily trigger clarification. Conversely, a planner could treat one executable candidate among several identity-distinct readings as the uniquely intended meaning.

**Recommended correction.** State the two tests separately:

1. Which analytical target does the request identify under governing declarations and any permitted single-valued default?
2. Which admitted and currently supported construction can establish that target?

Two ways to answer one question do not create two questions. A faster, cached, or available path does not by itself decide whether “average Revenue” meant Order or Customer. The candidate's own definition of distinct meanings already supports this refinement.

**Priority:** recommended acceptance clarification before planner-facing guidance.

## 8. Finding F6 — Restriction must be scoped relative to fixed contextual formation

**Observed.** LQ §2.4 distinguishes WHERE input restriction from HAVING output selection. O2 §19 describes forming ordered domains after request restriction. Neither provides the current manuscript's full distinction between restricting a result and silently reconstructing an already-constituted contextual operand under a different context.

**Comparison.** T §3.4's daily-change example and §4.2's participation rule require the same formation and contributions for a claimed continuation. A law can intentionally define formation under a supplied restriction, but that is a meaning-bearing contract choice.

**Risk, not a claim about current runtime behavior.** Moving a predicate into a predecessor or rolling calculation can change values by resetting its context. “Apply WHERE first” is not a universal authorization for that transformation.

**Recommended correction.** Preserve input restriction as the clause's jurisdiction and explicitly identify the formation scope to which it applies. Distinguish (a) selecting already-established contextual contributions, (b) a law that intentionally constructs within a restricted domain, and (c) an unsupported rewrite that changes formation. The 5-versus-20 daily-change example is sufficient to explain the risk; a new syntax is not needed to state the obligation.

**Priority:** semantic dependency clarification, not a new operator or query clause.

## 9. Finding F7 — R4's distinctions survive, but their application must stay target- and law-relative

**Observed.** R4 §2 explicitly says its dependency arrows are not an execution pipeline. Section 6 likewise makes placement failure dependency-specific. Its missingness definition and the corresponding LQ §8 are nevertheless earlier than the full manuscript's partial-evidence and materialization results.

**Comparison.** T §§2.3, 9, and 10.7 distinguish the target's point from contribution points, an unavailable basis from an unavailable target, and supported scalar L from retained W. Unknown contribution is not known-empty state.

**Recommended correction.** Retain the existence/placement/eligibility/support distinctions but specify which point and claim each statement concerns. Unsupported placement of one contributing point need not mean the requested output point does not exist. It can leave the output point established while making that target value unsupported. Conversely, missing earlier operand values need not block LAST when the intended domain, order, maximality, and selected value are established.

Add the full manuscript's guarded scalar-without-witness and coarser-without-finer-witness cases to the companion acceptance set. Repeat the scalar example's premises: fixed law, known nonemptiness, exhaustive possible-winner coverage, and supported equal values at every possible winner. Do not replace an unavailable intermediate witness by the empty state. Do not require a complete witness merely because it is one sufficient basis for a scalar target.

This is not permission for arbitrary approximate or partial answers. A disclosure must describe a justified claim; it does not make an undefined computation faithful. Exact missing-predicate syntax, enum design, and broad full-R4 implementation remain separate decisions.

**Priority:** required evidence/reuse clarification; no new standing ontology.

## 10. Finding F8 — O3 is the best starting point for order reconciliation, but is not yet the complete current contract

**Observed.** O3 already contains the agreed constituent-order-plus-precedence construction, sparse coordinate injection, original-point witness continuation, finite coherent-input premises, and a shared resolved-meaning requirement for explanation and execution. It explicitly qualifies the earlier universal locality claim. Its references still point to early amendments; its witness notation primarily describes a carrier rather than the full manuscript's W/L/R family relationships.

**Recommended correction.** Rebase O3's references and explain that T §8 is now authoritative for the witness-family construction. Keep O3 responsible for declaration, semantic comparison, identity binding, planner resolution, and realization. Do not duplicate and then independently evolve the four reviewed proofs.

Carry forward the materialization distinction, instance-relative coherent-input premise, and the limitation that a compressed winner is not a global input-consistency validator. Avoid a mandatory R→W→L storage pipeline. Retain order and context machinery for focal expressions where their laws need it; do not attach it indiscriminately to every ordered family.

The actual CDT contract still needs inspection. The source supports an outstanding interface obligation, not the factual claim that CDT lacks a particular type, and not a requirement to create a type literally named `Witness`.

**Priority:** targeted reference-and-contract reconciliation; retain most of O3.

## 11. Finding F9 — Preflight explanation must not promise unexamined execution evidence

**Observed.** WEB-L §1.7 describes a data-free EXPLAIN yet promises advance coverage of all execution disclosures. Its §2.1 also identifies canonical statement form with execution decomposition.

**Assessment.** The first promise is too strong unless every relevant data-dependent condition is established independently with applicable freshness. The second needs scope: preserving one analytical target does not require fixing one physical or sufficient-state plan.

**Recommended correction.** Separate canonical surface text, resolved analytical meaning, the proposed realization, and evidence actually established at that stage. EXPLAIN and execution must share the same meaning, not necessarily the same available plan across changed conditions. Report unresolved runtime obligations honestly rather than certify unexamined data. [WEB-L; compare O3 §11 and T §§5.5, 9, 12.4.]

**Priority:** reference-contract clarification. No runtime test or repair was performed here.

## 12. Finding F10 — Approximation and algorithm coverage remain separate from family admission

**Observed.** T §10.9 retains the same-target approximation boundary. Working capability migration is not an algorithm-law proof. WEB-K includes sketch-related entries and describes some exact operations as lacking finite witness state.

**Assessment.** A bounded-state limitation must not be confused with impossibility of exact finite retained state; T's multiset example already distinguishes those. Likewise a catalog entry for an approximate operation does not establish an exact basis or an admitted error contract.

**Recommended correction.** Preserve analytical target, exact/approximate guarantee, retained capability, and authorized reuse as separate claims. Qualify bounded-memory or implementation limitations accurately. Do not expand ToD's sketch catalog or implement algorithms during companion reconciliation. [WEB-K; T §§5, 10.9, 11.5.]

**Priority:** limited terminology/contract clarification, not a new approximation program.

## 13. Finding F11 — Published explanatory companions mostly need targeted successor edits

**Observed.** PI and PP's retrieved passages explain the result-declaration boundary, one current anchor, local @ versus frame AT, and the distinction between theory and shipped language. These do not repeat the working O2 categorical exclusion. PP's description of the Manifold environment groups analytical information and physical bindings together.

**Assessment.** The passages provide no reason to replace the introductions with an ordered-state treatise. There is a wording ambiguity between the overall governed execution environment and the logical-only authored Manifold. It is not evidence of an actual physical-authority leak.

**Recommended correction.** A successor should clarify the logical publication/private realization boundary, update theory/version pointers, and add a short note that an admitted family-forming law can give an expression analytical identity without making its spelling sufficient for admission. Preserve the existing @/AT teaching and the non-promotion role of output aliases.

A short order example in the Introduction can distinguish governed analytical points from physical rows. The Primer need not reproduce W, R, proof machinery, or the complete law contract. Both should state what their cited language/profile actually supports rather than import all of v7.1 into examples.

**Coverage limitation:** complete current publication source files and any later editions need a final whole-file check before successor publication. This assessment of their retrieved passages is not a certification of unexamined passages.

## 14. Finding F12 — Supersession must include acceptance tests, migration tables, and handoff instructions

**Observed.** AR, MM, CP, M2-I, and O2-I preserve the earlier categorical family boundary in different forms. Several mark it settled or use it to prescribe migration. Some of their source locations refer to earlier repository snapshots.

**Consequence.** A corrected Introduction does not make the working program safe if a developer or agent still receives an old instruction to remove FIRST/LAST family standing. Nor does a new document title supersede an incompatible acceptance expectation automatically.

**Recommended correction.** Publish one compact active-source index with exact versions and a disposition for each affected rule: retained, superseded, historical evidence, or unresolved. Do not erase prior research or edit the substance of old published versions to make them look current. Do not treat an old PASS verdict as a current endorsement of all premises used in that review.

Keep observations in dated reconnaissance reports as evidence at their recorded commits. Separately mark recommendations invalidated by theory succession. Public query spelling, legacy execution mechanism, admitted theory law, and present runtime conformance are different migration questions.

**Priority:** highest practical corpus-control action before another implementation handoff.

## 15. Public reference, profile, and build status: how to use them

WEB-L explicitly scopes its theoretical reconciliation to v6.1; retained syntax is not automatically a v7.1 contradiction. WEB-C and WEB-P are authored obligations, while WEB-B records build coverage. WEB-H is explicitly historical. Preserve those distinctions.

A coverage result in WEB-B is not a proof that all realizations satisfy T's ordered-family theorem. Conversely, a newly admitted theoretical law does not make its syntax or implementation available. A successor reference should state its theory alignment and separately point to grammar, profiles, and measured coverage. Do not hand-edit generated status to manufacture agreement. ## 16. Minimal reconciliation program

### Step 1 — Establish the active reading set

Use T as the working theoretical target. Mark O1/O2's categorical exclusion, OR's formation-locality exclusion, unfinished TOP-k promotion, and dependent migration instructions as superseded for present use. Preserve historical sources and observations. Do not send the old M2/O2 instructions unchanged.

### Step 2 — Produce one reconciled language-law candidate

Revise LQ around the current family contract, not around function-name classes. Correct its admission, promotion, evidence, canonicalization, and reuse passages; preserve broadcast, co-participation, WHERE/HAVING separation, and the request/execution boundary. Migrate acceptance cases with the text.

### Step 3 — Give each companion one job

O1 carries governed shorthand/completion principles after removing the categorical exclusion. O2 supplies focal domain/context/neighborhood analysis without deciding family admission by syntax or universal formation locality. O3 carries order declaration and realization and references T's W/L/R theory. R4 carries standing distinctions and law-relative dependencies, not a mandatory enum or universal all-input gate. CP/MM are then rebased as migration proposals, not executable instructions.

Do not create another set of overlapping full specifications merely to preserve every earlier filename.

### Step 4 — Make targeted introductory revisions

After the reconciled semantic account is stable, update the complete latest Introduction/Primer sources with short succession and authority clarifications. Keep the Primer short. Label theoretical notation and proposed syntax separately from accepted examples.

### Step 5 — Verify interfaces and implementations separately

Review actual CDT comparison/representation contracts, then code/grammar/profile coupling in a bounded engineering mission. Preserve the authority separation. Existing fail-closed containment work need not wait for full successor feature coverage, but this assessment authorizes no such changes.

## 17. Acceptance questions for the reconciled companion set

A current companion packet should answer all of these consistently:

1. Can a complete admitted canonical expression denote a family without AS or a business name? Yes; that does not grant query-time publication authority.
2. Does use of analytical order categorically preclude a family? No.
3. Does a FIRST/LAST spelling alone establish its law or conformance? No.
4. Is scalar L generally sufficient for W's continuation? No. Is W an admitted sufficient construction of L? Yes on its domain.
5. Must R be physically stored, or must every L argument establish W? No.
6. Do contextual ancestors automatically prohibit families? No; their actual formation and continuation contracts decide admission.
7. Do two adequate available bases create two meanings requiring clarification? No, when they realize one fixed target under compatible premises.
8. May availability alone choose between identity-distinct requested meanings? No.
9. Can a target be supported while one staged plan lacks an intermediate input? Yes; never substitute known-empty state for the unavailable input.
10. Can late withholding or disclosure retroactively validate an earlier undefined ordered computation? No.
11. Can WHERE or a changed grouping silently redefine fixed contextual formation? No.
12. Can query-level output sorting and limiting remain frame operations without becoming a family construction? Yes.
13. Does an approximate realization automatically create a new target family or exact sufficient-state standing? Neither follows automatically.
14. Does theoretical admission imply shipped grammar, profile coverage, or implementation conformance? No.
15. Are all earlier contradictory working rulings clearly identified as superseded? This must be checked across prose, examples, acceptance cases, and handoffs.

These are review questions, not an executed runtime test suite.

## 18. Final readiness judgment

**Frame-QL architectural direction:** retain.

**Current companion-corpus alignment:** incomplete; recent working rules contain direct conflicts and omissions relative to the reviewed manuscript.

**Need to reopen ToD's ontology from this comparison:** no new counterexample or missing foundational primitive was established by this read.

**Before presenting a unified current v7.1 companion packet:** identify supersession, provide one reconciled language-law target, correct its acceptance and reuse guidance, and make the theory/grammar/profile/evidence boundaries explicit.

**Not a prerequisite for finalizing the theory:** implementing every new expression, rewriting all historical publications, completing all sketch contracts, or creating a new CDT type named Witness.

The practical risk is a correct theory accompanied by an implementable but obsolete shortening of that theory. The remedy is to align the few statements that control admission, identity, evidence, and reuse—not to enlarge the core.
