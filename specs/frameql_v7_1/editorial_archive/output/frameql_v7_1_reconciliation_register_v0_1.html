3. Preserved versus revised

Preserved

The fixed ToD manuscript is copied unchanged into the review set. The Frame-QL request/result boundary, one final AT, expression-local @, constitutive inner anchors, co-participation, ordinary tuple/value structure, structural broadcast, and separation of WHERE/HAVING/output selection remain.

The Introduction retains all twelve numbered main sections and its existing 14 fenced Frame-QL examples. One subsection, §8.4, explains ordered families and retained capability. The Primer retains its original main section order and all three Frame-QL examples; it does not reproduce the witness proof. The exact averaging numbers and formulas remain. No example was re-executed.

O3 retains the actual two-level order definition, sparse coordinate geometry, declared constituent orders/precedence, and original-point witness idea. Its order-interface proposal does not become another theory of sufficient state.

Revised

The language contract now points directly to T’s specified-target requirement, formation/continuation distinction, and W/L/R account. Evidence and materialization passages include the complete guarded partial-evidence cases. The capability plan no longer prescribes a non-family FIRST/LAST category or changes a scalar re-entry flag by ontology. The public-reference patch sheet supplies proposed wording without editing generated facts.

The successor edition numbers for Introduction and Primer are proposed review labels, not new publications. The predecessor DOIs remain attributed only to the predecessor editions. No v7.1 DOI has been supplied or invented.

Fifteen existing local input artifacts were copied and hashed before editing. These include the theory target, earlier companions, plans, handoffs, assessment, and prior manifest. Their originals remain unchanged.

The page wrappers identify those as the hosted editions and state their own prior deposit-byte checks. That statement is not repeated here as an independent verification. Direct binary download into the container was unavailable. The local editorial inputs are full text transcriptions with normalized paragraph/blank-line whitespace and restored front matter from the complete web views. Their checksums identify those transcriptions, not the deposited originals.

This closes the prior passage-only reading limitation at the content level. It does not close a publication-production requirement for independently verified deposit bytes. The differences supplied for these two successors are against the archived transcriptions, not falsely labeled deposit-byte diffs.

Public Manual observations are also moving-main document observations rather than a commit-pinned implementation audit. The patch sheet is intentionally contextual replacement text, not a fabricated exact repository diff. Actual CDT v0.5 API readiness remains unverified.

5. Current checks and what they do not establish

The audit in this package checks source preservation, replay of recorded edits, Intro/Primer section order and Frame-QL code-block preservation, local link targets, mathematical delimiter/rendering integrity, presence of the required evidence guards and supersession dispositions, and consistency between the acceptance JSON and Markdown case set.

Those are document-integrity and editorial-consistency checks. They do not prove the new language semantics, execute the 40 semantic scenarios, validate a released build, or establish current data/certification completeness. The earlier mathematical test counts remain historical; none was rerun or advertised as new work.

The browser copies are rendered from the delivered Markdown with embedded styles and MathML. They are alternative reading formats, not separate authorities.
