## 1. The corrected conceptual model

### 1.1 Four layers, and the refinement that matters

**Manifold constitutes · Measure Algebra entails · MEL denotes · Frame-QL requests.** Only the first verb
creates anything; authority passes downhill only.

The refinement that reorganizes everything below: **MEL can fix the analytical structure of**

```text
mean(X @ day) @ week
```

**without knowing what `X` is.** The structure — one MEAN family, constitutive input at day, current anchor at
week, a \((\Sigma,N)\) sufficient state, a nonempty-fiber contract — is the same whether `X` is revenue,
balance, or temperature.

That is a **parametricity** claim, and taking it seriously is the whole rework. If the structure is parametric
in `X`, then everything the construction needs from `X` is confined to what the law actually consumes. So the
right question is never *"what does `X` forbid?"* but:

> **What does this law consume, and from where?**

MEAN consumes `X`'s **values at its constitutive input anchor**, under `X`'s **participation rule**, and
requires those values to support its **operations**. Three premises, all discharged at one location.

**None of them is "X may be composed across day."** MEAN never asks `X` to move. It reads `X` where `X`
lives and applies a *different* law to the collection it finds.

> **`balance` cannot cross time. `mean(balance @ day)` never asks it to.** That is the whole answer, and it
> needed no propagation rule — because there was never a movement of `balance` to license.

### 1.2 Two kinds of authority (retained from v0.1 §1.4)

**Constitutive** authority is held by a person, concerns a contingent world, could have been exercised
otherwise; it is ratifiable, versionable and *wrong-able*. **Nomological** authority is held by nobody,
concerns every world, could not have been otherwise, and is not ratifiable — ratifying a theorem adds nothing
to it. ToD §4 states the three-way and defines each authority by what it cannot do:

> A declaration states a proposed definition. **Human ratification establishes the declaration's authority in
> its domain; it does not prove its mathematical laws.** A proof establishes a conditional analytical
> statement; **it does not certify that a particular source currently satisfies its premises.** A backend
> computation establishes neither merely by returning a value.

Every defect found in this estate is an attempted conversion between the two. The rework below is an attempt
to stop performing one: **a constructed family's domain is nomological content, and we had been asking a
human to declare it.**

### 1.3 Two claims from v0.1 I now withdraw

Stated plainly, per instruction.

**(a) Withdrawn: "participation intent is a residual constitutive fact of a construction."** v0.1 §1.4.3
listed it among three facts MEL cannot supply, on the strength of ToD's 100-vs-97 example. **That reading was
wrong.** §11.5.1 settles participation for a construction *from its operand*:

> `count(I)` counts participating analytical points of anchor \(I\), each contributing one. **`count(x@I)`
> counts participation under the operand construction's governed rule.** These are distinct targets.

So `count(order)` = 100 and `count(revenue@order)` = 97 are **two different expressions**, both spellable in
MEL, each with determinate participation. The *"intended 100-order MEAN"* ToD warns about is not a reading of
`mean(revenue@order)` — it is a different construction (a ratio over `count(order)`), and MEL can denote it.
So 100-vs-97 is a **request-level ambiguity that Frame-QL must Clarify**, not a constitutive gap. Participation
is authored **once, on the observational family**, and entailed through every construction above it.

**(b) Qualified: "MAP1 is the wrong relation, the wrong operator class, and the wrong object."** The
criticism stands *as a criticism of using MAP1 to propagate domains*. But it mislocated MAP1. MAP1's actual
content is \(E'=\bigcap_i E_i,\ S'=\bigcap_i S_i\) — **eligibility and support intersection**. That is a
**co-participation rule**, and §1.5 below shows co-participation is a genuine residual for multi-input
constructions. **MAP1 is the right rule for a question we were not asking**, offered conservatively (*"It does
not prove that all future multi-input laws must use strict intersection participation"*). Its \(\beta'\) union
remains the wrong object for a family domain; its intersections are a candidate default for a real premise.

### 1.4 The reformulation: a domain is where the law has an argument

This replaces v0.1's A6 and, with it, \(P_F\).

> \[ \mathcal A_F=\{\,A:\ F\text{'s own law has an argument at }A\,\} \]

§4.1's demand — *"The family's definition must license the quantity being claimed there"* — is met by an
argument existing, and by nothing else. ToD supplies exactly two kinds of argument:

**(i) Continuation.** \(F\)'s own values fold over the \(A\)-fiber. Note ToD's definition is **already
scope-relative**: §5.2 — *"A family is self-sufficient when its own supported measure values compose **across
admitted refinement** by an associative and commutative continuation law."* The law and the refinements it
covers are **one object in ToD's own formulation.**

**(ii) Construction.** An admitted basis is established at \(A\) and the constructor is defined there. §5.1 —
*"The constructor is **anchor-local**: its required inputs are the already-established measures \(G_i@A\) at
the target anchor."*

Now the two cases diverge completely, and that divergence is the result.

**For a family standing by continuation,** the scope of the fold is a **contingent semantic fact**:

\[ \mathcal A_F=\{A:\ A_0\succeq A,\ \operatorname{Forgotten}(A_0\to A)\subseteq \mathrm{Fold}_F\} \]

where \(\mathrm{Fold}_F\) is the set of constituents over whose fibers \(\oplus_F\) is **meaningful**. This is
the same *set* the prohibition formulation produced, with the polarity inverted — and the inversion is not
cosmetic:

- The primitive is **positive** and belongs **with the law**, where ToD §5.2 already puts it.
- **The three-state polarity problem evaporates.** There is no *"established with \(P_F=\varnothing\)"*
  versus *"absent"* distinction to protect, because a family either has a continuation law (which comes with a
  scope) or it does not. Two states. *"Absence of prohibition is not permission"* becomes **trivially true**:
  no declared fold means no fold, hence \(\mathcal A_F=\{A_0\}\).

**For a family standing by construction, the domain is entailed:**

\[ \mathcal A_F=\{A: A_0\succeq A\}\ \cap\ \bigcup_{\mathcal B}\ \bigcap_{G\in\mathcal B}\mathcal A_G,
\qquad A_0=I \]

— union over admitted bases (§5.5 permits several with different domains), intersection over the roles of
each. **\(A_0=I\) is forced, not chosen:** the constructor's inputs are established at \(I\) and nothing
finer is expressible, so the finest anchor at which the law has an argument *is* \(I\). This **dissolves
v0.1's D-2**, which asked whether \(A_0\) is authored for a construction. It is not.

ToD states this coupling in the one case v7.1 works out — §8.1:

> Every target covered by that basis claim must admit the corresponding \(W@A\); **a more restricted
> witness-family domain would yield a correspondingly restricted basis claim.**

Recursion terminates at observational leaves by §5.4 well-foundedness.

### 1.5 What \(P_F\) was actually doing — two jobs, now separated

\(P_F\) conflated two facts with different natures, which is why its identity standing needed a ruling.

| | what it is | identity-bearing? | where it goes now |
|---|---|---|---|
| **semantic scope** | the fold is *meaningless* over this constituent — balances do not add across days | **yes** — it is part of the continuation law, and §3.9 makes that a succession trigger | into the continuation law, positively, as \(\mathrm{Fold}_F\) |
| **governance prohibition** | the fold is *meaningful but not permitted* — §A.2.3's own example, a Revenue family *"later prohibited from composing away a day constituent"* | **no** | a separate overlay, adjacent to coverage permission; **not a family-identity fact** |

This is why §3.9 (continuation law ⇒ succession) and §A.2.3 (domain law ⇒ no succession) read as a tension
that A.2.3 had to resolve by fiat — *"'Part of the law' does not mean 'part of immutable family identity.'"*
**Both rulings are right about different facts.** Separating them removes the need for the fiat, and §A.1.8's
own instinct was already correct in the adjacent case: coverage permission *"is not part of \(P_F\)."*

### 1.6 The residuals, stated before the examples

Testing rather than presupposing, the answer is **neither** *inherit* **nor** *author*:

| construction | authored facts it adds |
|---|---|
| single operand, catalogued law (`sum`, `mean`, `count`, `min`, `max`, `distinct_set`, `multiset`) | **none** |
| **multi-input** (AOV, covariance) | **the co-participation contract** — §3.5 requires operands *"lawfully co-established under the applicable universe, type, and **co-participation contract**"*, and whether two operands count the same points is contingent |
| **order-dependent** (FIRST/LAST) | **a reference to a governed order**, where the world admits more than one (§7.2) |
| **bespoke law**, not in the catalogue | **the target specification**, plus bases and their §5.3 adequacy |

> **The Manifold declares a world, its observational quantities, and — where needed — laws and orders. It does
> not declare families. Families are denoted.**

That is *"the Manifold declares the generators"* taken literally and followed through.

## 2. Observational `revenue`

### 2.1 The world

```
universe sales
  identity     : sales
  individuates : { store, day, sale }     # closed; each with governed ref + value domain
  exists_when  : a sale was transacted at that store on that day
```

Derived and therefore absent: \(\Omega\), \(R_{sales}=\{store*day*sale\}\), `{}`, every sub-anchor and its
fiber partition, the refinement order. `@ sale` in an expression is shorthand that resolves to \(R_{sales}\)
**only where the constitution makes `{sale}` = \(R_{sales}\)** — itself a derived equality (Ruling 3), never
an asserted one.

### 2.2 The smallest declaration

```
family revenue
  in            : sales
  quantity      : the consideration accruing from the participating sale-points
                  of the target fiber
  root          : { store, day, sale }
  participation : every eligible root point contributes once
  values        : amount in a governed currency; requires + , = , 0
  continuation  : monoid ( +, 0 )  folding over { store, day, sale }
```

**Six facts. That is the whole of it**, and every one could have been otherwise.

### 2.3 Classification

| fact | class | why |
|---|---|---|
| universe, root, quantity, participation, values, continuation-with-scope | **AUTHORED** | none is entailed by any law; a world could have observed revenue per-line-item, counted differently, or measured in units that do not add |
| \(\mathcal A_{revenue}\) = all eight anchors \(A\preceq A_0\) | **ENTAILED** | §1.4(i): the fold covers every constituent |
| direct/staged agreement over `{store,day,sale} → {store,day} → {store}` | **ENTAILED** | Proposition 6.1 |
| empty-fiber value = the monoid identity | **ENTAILED** | §6.1 with the empty-state contract |
| `revenue`, `revenue @ {store}` as identities | **DENOTED** | MEL; `@` is ascription (Frame-QL §3.2) |
| which anchor a caller wants; `AT {region}` | **REQUESTED** | Frame-QL |
| support \(S_{F,A}\), coverage, availability | **EVIDENCE** | §2.3, §1.5 |
| tables, columns, plans, carriers, precision | **REALIZATION** | §12.3; Frame-QL §1.3 |

**What makes Revenue the same family at other anchors.** Nothing in its six facts mentions an anchor other
than \(A_0\). `revenue @ {store}` is one measure of one family, licensed by the fold. Frame-QL is explicit
that it does **not** become a construction: *"It does not need to become `sum(revenue @ {transaction}) @
{region}` merely because Revenue happens to have an additive family law. The query names the family
identity."*

## 3. Observational `balance`

### 3.1 The world

```
universe ledger
  identity     : ledger
  individuates : { account, day }
  exists_when  : the account is open on that day        # GENERATIVE, not occurrence-based
```

\(\lambda_{ledger}\) must be generative — a balance exists on a day with no transactions. Ruling 7b requires
the constitution reach this case, and a restriction over an occurrence population cannot express it.

### 3.2 The smallest declaration

```
family balance
  in            : ledger
  quantity      : the amount standing to the account at the CLOSE OF THE DAY
  root          : { account, day }
  participation : every eligible root point contributes once
  values        : amount in a governed currency; requires + , = , 0
  continuation  : monoid ( +, 0 )  folding over { account }        # <-- the only difference
```

**Structurally identical to Revenue. One field differs**, and it is the field the whole Balance/Revenue
contrast lives in.

### 3.3 The contrast, and why it is contingent

\(\mathrm{Fold}_{revenue}=\{store,day,sale\}\) and \(\mathrm{Fold}_{balance}=\{account\}\). Entailed:

\[ \mathcal A_{balance}=\{\{account,day\},\ \{day\}\} \]

Balance stands at its root and at `{day}` — the total balance across open accounts on a day — and **nowhere
else**.

**Why the scope is a property of the target, not an extra field.** Look at the two `quantity` lines. Revenue's
reads *"…of the participating sale-points of the target fiber"* — it is **fiber-uniform**: it says what the
quantity is for *any* fiber, so the fold covers everything. Balance's reads *"…at the close of the day"* — it
**names a location**. At `{account}` there is no *"the day"*, so the target does not denote there; it is not
prohibited, it is **undefined**.

> **A target stated uniformly over the fiber entails a total fold. A target that names a location entails a
> fold that cannot cross it.** `continuation`'s scope is the machine-checkable face of `quantity`.

That is why it is authored rather than derived: a target specification is prose, so a gate cannot compute the
scope from it — but a human reviewer can check the two against each other, and an inconsistency between them
is a *well-formedness* defect rather than an unadjudicable one.

**And whether balances add across accounts is genuinely open to the author.** Mixed currencies,
asset/liability sign conventions and intercompany positions all defeat it. An author who declines writes
`continuation : none`, and then \(\mathcal A_{balance}=\{A_0\}\). **Both are lawful; the model does not
choose.**

### 3.4 Note the two guards, and which does the work

`balance @ {account, week}` is refused twice over, and it matters that the *first* reason is semantic:

1. **`quantity` does not denote there** — there is no single day.
2. **No argument exists** — the fold cannot cross `day`, and `balance` has no basis.
3. And were someone to propose *"sum the daily balances"* as a repair, **§5.3 refuses it independently**: that
   construction is establishable and deterministic but *"SUM and MAX can each be deterministic and coherent
   while denoting different quantities"* — it does not construct this target.

This is why removing a *governance* prohibition (§1.5) could never turn "balance" into "sum of daily
balances": the semantics are carried by `quantity`, and the domain rule merely records their consequence.

### 3.5 The cross-time quantities exist — they are other families

| the question | the family | licence at `{account, week}` |
|---|---|---|
| closing balance for the week | `last(balance @ {account,day})` under a governed day order | construction, witness monoid (§8.1) |
| average daily balance | `mean(balance @ {account,day})` | construction, \((\Sigma,N)\) basis |
| the balance itself | `balance` | **none — refused** |

## 4. `sum(revenue @ sale)` and `mean(revenue @ sale)`

**Additional Manifold constitution required: none, for either.**

### 4.1 `sum(revenue @ sale)`

```
DENOTED (MEL)   : SUM( revenue @ R_sales )
ENTAILED        : root A_0 = R_sales
                  target  = the additive total of the participating sale-level
                            revenue values in the fiber            # from the SUM law
                  participation = revenue's rule                   # §11.5.1
                  continuation  = the additive law, folding over EVERY constituent
                  domain  = all eight anchors
AUTHORED        : nothing
```

**Why its fold is total while `balance`'s is not, without anyone declaring it.** The SUM law's target is
fiber-uniform *by construction* — "the total of what is in the fiber" is a statement about an arbitrary fiber.
So §3.3's criterion applies mechanically and the scope is **entailed from the law**, not authored. This is the
precise sense in which a construction needs no domain declaration.

**Is it the same family as `revenue`?** Possibly, and that is a **naming** question, not a new family.
§11.5.1: *"A named family such as Revenue may already denote the same construction; canonicalization can
identify them only where identity and participation agree."* Where they agree, §3.9's governed default
completion may let `revenue` resolve to it — a naming act, non-constitutive.

### 4.2 `mean(revenue @ sale)`

```
DENOTED (MEL)   : MEAN( revenue @ R_sales )
ENTAILED        : root A_0 = R_sales
                  target  = arithmetic mean of participating sale-level revenues
                            in the fiber                           # from the MEAN law
                  participation = revenue's rule                   # §11.5.1 -- NOT a residual
                  continuation  = NONE (a mean does not continue from a mean, §5.2)
                  bases   = { sum(revenue@R), count(revenue@R) }    # ToD Appendix A
                  domain  = all eight anchors, via those bases
                  empty   = undefined (nonempty constructor domain)
AUTHORED        : nothing
REQUESTED       : the current anchor; and Clarify if the caller said
                  "average revenue" without fixing the constitutive input anchor
```

**The 100-vs-97 case, correctly placed.** `mean(revenue@sale)` has a determinate denominator — revenue's
participation. The *other* quantity, a mean over all sale points including unsupported ones, is a **different
MEL expression** (a ratio over `count(sale)`). Both are lawful; both are denotable. So this is a **Clarify**,
and §8 below says so. It is not a missing authored fact — which is the correction in §1.3(a).

## 5. `mean(balance @ day)` — what additional constitution is required?

> **None.**

```
DENOTED (MEL)   : MEAN( balance @ {account, day} )
ENTAILED        : root A_0 = { account, day }                      # = I, forced (§1.4)
                  target  = arithmetic mean of the participating daily balances
                            over the day-points of the target fiber
                  participation = balance's rule                   # §11.5.1
                  continuation  = NONE                             # §5.2
                  bases   = { sum(balance@I), count(balance@I) }   # ToD Appendix A
                  domain  = every anchor coarser than { account, day }
                  empty   = undefined
AUTHORED        : nothing
```

**Why `balance`'s restriction does not reach it — the short form.** The MEAN law never moves `balance`. It
reads `balance` at `{account,day}`, which is exactly where `balance` lives, and applies a different law to the
values it finds. There is no movement of `balance` to license, so there is nothing for
\(\mathrm{Fold}_{balance}=\{account\}\) to refuse.

**Why its own domain is total.** Its basis roles are `sum(balance@I)` and `count(balance@I)` — two **ordinary
families** whose targets are fiber-uniform by construction (§4.1), so their folds cover every constituent, so
the basis is established at `{account}`, `{day}` and `{}`. Hence
\(\mathcal A_F=\{A:A_0\succeq A\}\) and **`mean(balance@day)` stands at `{account}`** — the average daily
balance per account, which `balance` itself cannot reach.

**The reductio, retained from v0.1 §5.5 and now with a cleaner explanation.** Had the construction inherited
\(\{day\}\), it could not forget `day`, so it could not stand at `{account}` — *the only thing it exists to
produce.* Under this model the question cannot even be posed: **there is nothing to inherit, because a
construction has no fold scope of its own to restrict.** Its standing comes from its basis, and its basis
consists of different quantities.

The governing diagnosis stands, and is now a consequence rather than a discovery:

> **A formation law creates a different analytical quantity. Facts governing continuation of the operand are
> not automatically facts governing the resulting quantity.**

## 6. `mean(mean(balance @ day) @ week)` — what additional constitution is required?

> **No additional *family* constitution. But one contingent *universe* fact decides whether the outer
> roll-up is meaningful, and it is not a fact about families at all.**

### 6.1 The two families, kept apart

Per the correction: the outer expression does **not** ask \(M_1\) to continue from week to month. It **consumes
\(M_1@week\) as the constitutive input to a new family-forming operation.**

```
M1 = MEAN( balance @ {account, day} )          A_0 = {account, day}
     M1 @ {account, week}                      a measure of M1 -- admitted, §5

M2 = MEAN( M1 @ {account, week} )              A_0 = {account, week}   <-- I, forced
     M2 @ ...                                  a measure of M2
```

\(M_2\)'s entailments run exactly as \(M_1\)'s did: target from the MEAN law (*the mean of the participating
weekly average daily balances over the week-points of the fiber*), participation from \(M_1\)'s rule,
continuation none, bases \(\{sum(M_1@I_2),count(M_1@I_2)\}\), domain from those bases. **Authored: nothing.**

This confirms the correction directly: `mean(mean(...))` is **not** a mean-of-means pathology. It is a lawful
second formation, and it is a **different quantity** from `mean(balance@day)@month` — the unweighted mean of
weekly means versus the mean over all days. Both are meaningful. §3.7's child-state warning — states
\((100,1)\) and \((300,2)\) combine to \(400/3\), *"not the unweighted mean \(125\) of the displayed child
ratios"* — is not a prohibition on the second construction; it is the statement that the two are **different
targets**, which is precisely what makes them both declarable.

### 6.2 The constraint I did not expect, and it is ToD's own example

\(M_2\)'s root is \(\{account,week\}\), so its domain is the anchors **coarser** than that: `{account}`,
`{week}`, `{}`. **`{account, month}` is not among them** — it is not a subset. And ToD §2.1.2 names exactly
this case:

> Refinement is a partial order. Two anchors can both be governed without either refining the other.
> **Calendar Week and Calendar Month are familiar examples: Day can refine each, while a week can cross a
> month boundary.** A valid weekly materialization does not thereby establish exact calendar-month values.

So `mean(mean(balance @ day) @ week) @ month` turns on a **contingent fact about the world's calendar**:

- **If the world's weeks nest in its months** (a 4-4-5 retail calendar, or quarters of thirteen whole weeks),
  then `week → month` is a governed projection, the constitutive input anchor is \(\{account,week,month\}\) —
  `month` adds nothing to the inner quantity — and the roll-up is exact.
- **If they do not** (ISO weeks over calendar months), `{account,week}` and `{account,month}` are
  **incomparable**. §6.3: *"The family guarantees agreement along admitted paths; **it does not manufacture a
  path between incomparable locations**."* Forcing it would silently change the inner quantity from *a weekly
  mean* to *a mean over the part of the week falling inside the month* — which §4.2 forbids in terms:
  *"A staged proof must combine the same intended contributions, with the same multiplicity and formation, as
  its direct counterpart."*

> **The right disposition when weeks do not nest is Refuse, and the reason is nameable: there is no governed
> projection `week → month`.** That is not a family defect, not a propagation question, and not an ambiguity.

The same fact decides `@ quarter`: lawful where a quarter is a whole number of weeks.

## 7. Multi-input: average order value

```
universe sales
  individuates : { customer, day, order, line }
  exists_when  : a line was transacted
```

```
family revenue        root { customer, day, order, line }   fold { all }     # as §2
family order_count    DENOTED: COUNT( {customer, day, order} )               # count of order points
```

```
family average_order_value
  DENOTED (MEL)  : revenue / order_count            # a ratio law, nominated as the defining construction
  ENTAILED       : root A_0 = the common anchor of the operands
                   target  = the nominated quotient, §3.7
                   domain  = where both operands stand      # anchor-local, §5.1
                   the child-state discipline: (100,1),(300,2) -> 400/3        # §3.7, a theorem
  AUTHORED       : *** the co-participation contract ***
```

**This is the one genuinely new authored fact in the whole set, and it is not a domain fact.** §3.5 requires
operands *"lawfully co-established under the applicable universe, type, and **co-participation contract**"*,
and adds the reason it cannot be inferred: *"Equal coordinate spelling alone does not establish all these
premises."* §5.3 repeats it — *"Individually supported inputs drawn from incompatible populations do not form
an admitted basis merely because their types match."*

Concretely: does the denominator count **every eligible order**, or **only orders carrying revenue**? An order
with no revenue line, and a revenue line on a cancelled order, are both real, and the two readings give
different quantities. **Nothing in the expression or in either operand settles it.**

**And this is where MAP1 actually belongs** (§1.3(b)). MAP1's content is \(E'=\bigcap E_i\), \(S'=\bigcap
S_i\) — it is a **conservative default co-participation rule**, offered as one candidate: *"It does not prove
that all future multi-input laws must use strict intersection participation."* Adopting it as the default here
would be lawful and would be a **governed default, declared once**, not a derivation. Its \(\beta'\) union
remains the wrong object for a domain.

## 8. Ordered contrast: `last(balance @ day)`

```
family closing_balance
  DENOTED (MEL)  : LAST( balance @ {account, day} ; order )
  ENTAILED       : root A_0 = { account, day }
                   target  = the value at the governed-latest participating day-point
                   witness family W and the basis {W} -> L                 # §8.1
                   continuation  = the witness monoid, over EVERY constituent  # §8.2-8.3
                   domain  = every anchor coarser than A_0
                   empty   = the known-empty witness state, bottom            # §8.2
  AUTHORED       : *** a reference to a governed order, where the world admits several ***
```

Two facts, at two levels, and the split is the point:

- **Universe level, AUTHORED:** that the `day` constituent carries a governed complete order. §7.3 refuses any
  substitute — *"Appending a storage identifier or relying on sort stability is not a repair of analytical
  law."*
- **Family level:** *which* order, where more than one exists. §7.2 — *"Different order definitions over the
  same anchor may coexist; **the family must resolve which one it uses**"* — and it is **identity-bearing**.
  **Entailed where the world admits exactly one; authored where it admits several.**

**The instructive part is what is *not* authored.** `last(balance@day)` stands at `{account}`, `{day}` and `{}`
— **a wider domain than `balance` itself** — because the *witness monoid* folds over every constituent
(Proposition 8.1), even though `balance`'s own fold does not cross `day`. The ordered case therefore makes the
same point as MEAN, with a different law: **the operand's fold scope is simply not the constructed family's
fold scope.** Nothing carried across, and nothing needed to.

## 10. The proposed declaration shape

Illustrative. Shaped so every entailed fact is **unstatable**.

```
universe <identity>
  individuates : { <constituent> : <governed ref, value domain> , ... }   # closed
  exists_when  : <λ_U>                                                   # determinate
  ordered      : { <constituent> : <governed complete order> , ... }     # only where needed
  projects     : { <constituent> -> <constituent> }                      # only where it holds

placement <identity>                       # Case G only
  on : <universe>   construction : <governed construction>
  kind : assignment | membership-universe | allocation | full-touch

family <identity>                          # OBSERVATIONAL ONLY
  in            : <universe>
  quantity      : <target specification>
  root          : <A_0>
  participation : <rule, including multiplicity at one root point>
  values        : <semantic requirements>
  continuation  : <monoid|semigroup> folding over { <constituents> }     # or: none
```

**There is no `family` block for a construction.** A construction is a **MEL expression**, and the Manifold
carries only what an expression cannot: a **co-participation contract** where a law takes several operands, an
**order reference** where a world admits several, and a **target plus bases** where a law is not catalogued.
Those attach to the *law application*, not to a pre-registered family:

```
law <identity>                              # only for bespoke or multi-input laws
  form          : <MEL expression schema>
  co_participation : <contract>              # multi-input only
  target        : <specification>            # bespoke only
  bases         : <role-indexed> + adequacy  # bespoke only

name <token> -> <MEL identity>               # optional, non-constitutive
```

Five properties:

1. **No `kind`, no lineage block, no `admitted_anchors`, no `domain`, no `prohibited_constituents`, no
   agreement clause, no derived value domains.** Each is entailed, and admitting one creates *"a surface on
   which they can state it falsely."*
2. **`continuation` carries its own scope, positively.** Two states — a law with a scope, or `none` — not
   three. *"Absence of prohibition is not permission"* becomes trivially true.
3. **`quantity` and `continuation`'s scope must agree**, and that is a well-formedness obligation a reviewer
   can check (§3.3).
4. **Families are not enumerated.** The Manifold is finite; the family space is not.
5. **Order and projection live on the universe**, because they are facts about constituents, not quantities.

## 12. Prior rulings that answered the wrong question

Stated plainly, as invited. **The discoveries are preserved; the architecture changes.**

**C3-as-shipped answered the wrong question.** `prohibited_constituents`, the two-level polarity, and
`CONSTRUCTED_DOMAIN_UNDECIDED` all presuppose that a constructed family's analytical domain is a **declared
fact**. Under §1.4 it is **entailed**. So:

- **There is no constructed-family domain declaration to get right.** The producer/consumer gap — 13 keys
  versus 14 — is not a bug to fix but a **field to delete**.
- **`CONSTRUCTED_DOMAIN_UNDECIDED` has nothing to refuse for.** It was reading an entailment as a gap.
- **`P_F` survives only for observational families**, in **positive** form, **merged into the continuation
  law**. The earlier open question *"positive or negative content inside an established law?"* resolves to
  positive, for the reason in §1.4.
- **The three-state polarity apparatus is unnecessary.** Two states suffice.

**And one ruling is partly reversed.** §A.2.3 ruled \(P_F\) **not identity-bearing**. Under §1.5 that is
**right about the governance overlay and wrong about the semantic scope**: the fold scope *is* part of the
continuation law, and §3.9 makes a changed continuation law a **succession trigger**. Changing
\(\mathrm{Fold}_{balance}\) from `{account}` to `{account, day}` is not a permission change — it asserts that
balances add across time, which is **a different quantity**. The apparent tension A.2.3 resolved by fiat
(*"'Part of the law' does not mean 'part of immutable family identity'"*) was the symptom of one field doing
two jobs.

**What survives intact:** the falsification of operand-domain propagation (now a consequence, not a
discovery); the split of family-domain from edge-validity; *"absence of prohibition is not permission"* (now
trivially true); one family kind; lineage and C6 derived; record totality's *"lawful entailment from other
established parts"*, which is exactly the mechanism this model relies on; and the placement of the family
reducer as a governed fact rather than a realization one.

## 13. What remains open

**Dissolved by this rework:** v0.1's **D-2** (\(A_0\) for constructions — forced to \(I\)) and **D-3** (may a
construction tighten \(P_F\) — there is no \(P_F\)). **D-4** shrinks: the A.1/A.2 apparatus need not be
adopted, because the model does not use it.

**Still open, and now better located:**

- **O-1 · Calendar geometry.** Two questions, both contingent universe facts, both unresolved: is `day → week`
  constituent-forgetting or a Case-G placement (v0.1 D-1, and Case G is held); and does the world's `week`
  project onto its `month` (§6.2). The second is **not** a held boundary — it is an ordinary authored fact the
  model now has a slot for (`projects`), and naming it is the smaller of the two wins.
- **O-2 · Where does the fold scope live in the theory?** §5.2 already says *"admitted refinement"* but does
  not say what fixes it. This model puts it in the continuation law. That needs a home in a successor edition —
  and it is a **smaller** ask than adopting \(P_F\) and its polarity.
- **O-3 · The governance-prohibition overlay.** §1.5 separates it out; §A.1.8 leaves coverage permission's
  *"governing location… not assigned"*. These two belong together and neither is placed.
- **O-4 · Default co-participation.** Adopt MAP1's intersection as the governed default for multi-input laws,
  or require it per family? A default would be lawful and would reduce the only recurring authored cost.
  Recommend adopting it as a **declared** default, so it is visible rather than assumed.
- **O-5 · Non-monotone domains** (v0.1 D-5, unchanged). A constituent-set fold scope makes \(\mathcal A_F\)
  downward closed, so *"may drop `store`, but not all the way to `{}`"* stays unstatable. ToD's own notation
  \(\Gamma_F(B\to A)\) is edge-indexed. Recorded; not answered.
- **O-6 · `SOURCE` + governed default completion** (v0.1 D-7). Still an option; less pressing now that
  participation is entailed rather than residual.

**v0.1 D-6** (contribution intake at a root point) is **resolved by placement**: it is `participation` on the
observational family, and §10's shape says so explicitly.
