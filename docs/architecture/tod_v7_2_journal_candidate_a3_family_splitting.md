# Candidate entry A.3 for the v7.2 development journal — family splitting, recovered from Version 6.1

**Standing.** A **candidate entry**, drafted by Claude at Huayin's direction, 2026-09-24, for adjudication into
`tod_v7_2_development_addendum_v0_1.md`. It is written in that document's form. **It is not adopted, carries no
authority, and is deliberately filed separately rather than edited into an authored document.**

**Why it is recorded.** The principle below was located during the B-anchor archaeology. It is very close to the
positive theory the September 2026 development work rediscovered independently, and it was **already published**
in Version 6.1. Huayin's direction: *recover the principle, not necessarily the old machinery.*

---

## A.3.1 Subject

Version 7.1 states that formation can change family identity (§3.1), that constitutive anchors and law
parameters remain inside \(F\) (§2.2), and that a changed identity-bearing definition requires succession
(§3.9). It does **not** state the converse-facing principle that makes those sentences usable in practice:
**what happens when one ordinary-language label names two analytically different quantities.**

Version 6.1 stated it directly.

## A.3.2 The principle, verbatim from Version 6.1

> **If two analytical directions require genuinely different coherent reducer laws, ToD assigns them distinct
> family identities even when everyday language reuses one label.** The distinct coherent regions are connected
> through lineage. **Ordinary language may call both quantities "inventory,"** for example; governed canonical
> names distinguish them when their analytical identities differ.

Version 6.1 pairs it with an explicit three-way placement:

> identity-bearing contracts belong in \(\Sigma(F)\); edge-validity predicates in \(\Gamma(e)\); approximation,
> retained capability, and realization quality in the certificate/materialization layer

## A.3.3 What it disposes of

Version 6.1 carries **no** blocked-axis mechanism: no \(\beta\), no `Spent`, no B-anchor. ⟨measured⟩ the word
`blocked` appears **zero times** in Version 7.1, in every Version 7.0 draft, and in the v2.3 primer and
introduction. The inherited Contract Calculus boundary map \(\beta:\mathsf{AggCap}\to\mathcal P(\mathsf{Axis})\)
was **not carried forward**; its work was split between the principle above (identity) and \(\Gamma(e)\) (edge
validity).

Version 6.1 also restates the fan-out case without \(\beta\):

> The proposed continuation violates the multiplicity contract of the Revenue lineage edge, leaving the
> resulting 300 uncertified as `revenue@tag`.

## A.3.4 Why it matters now

The September 2026 development work reached the same position from the opposite direction, and the two meet
exactly:

> `sum(stock @ day) @ year` can be a number while `stock @ year` is not established.

The first denotes a construction whose law determines a value over that fiber. The second asks for Stock itself,
and Stock's law determines nothing there. **They are different families, and ordinary language calls both
"stock."** That is A.3.2, instantiated.

**The practical consequence is the better account of semi-additivity:**

> **Arithmetic is not illegal. Arithmetic simply does not get to rename its result as the original quantity.**

This retires the stock/flow/semi-additive taxonomy without needing a prohibition mechanism — and two independent
authorities already refuse that taxonomy on their own grounds: ADR-036 D6 (*"No stock/flow/rating type"*) and
CDT v0.5 (*"It does not need a separate folklore taxonomy such as 'additive metric,' 'semi-additive metric,' or
'non-additive metric.'"*).

## A.3.5 What this entry does **not** propose

* **It does not restore \(\beta\).** The capability-indexed boundary map answers *"which distinctions may
  capability \(\kappa\) not spend?"* — a question about an **operation**. A family-indexed prohibition set
  answers *"at which anchors may \(F\) exist?"* — a question about a **quantity**. Dropping \(\kappa\) does not
  weaken the first question into the second; **it changes the type of the question.**
* **It does not restore \(\mathcal C_L\)** or the draft4 three-level factorization, notwithstanding that its
  deletion in rev1 removed the vocabulary separating law-level meaning from local prohibition from realization.
  A simpler reconstruction is available and should be derived rather than resurrected:
  **law determines meaning → governance may withhold → evidence/profile determines servability.**
* **It does not propose an authored family-domain object.** \(\mathcal A_F\) is not needed as an independently
  constituted, serialized or governed object. The *notation* survives descriptively as the extension of a
  partial law, \(\mathcal A_F=\{A: F@A \text{ is defined by the law of } F\}\) — **eliminating an authored
  family domain does not require eliminating the mathematical fact that a partial law has a domain.**

## A.3.6 Open, and not decided here

* The **family-construction / law-synthesis** question: *given a lawful construction over existing measures,
  what law does the resulting family have?* This is the genuine successor to what was filed as prohibition
  propagation, and it is no longer a propagation problem. `mean`, ratios, differences, covariance and LAST all
  belong to it.
* The **non-semantic restriction** question: once analytical meaning is established, what kinds of restriction
  may prevent serving it? `WITHHOLD`, coverage permission and authorization need sorting **without being
  allowed to redefine analytical meaning.**
